Materials After the Engineered Stone Ban

The choice of benchtop material has been significantly impacted by the ban on engineered stone containing 1 percent or more crystalline silica, effective across Australia from 1 July 2024. This ban affects its manufacture, supply, processing, and installation. Consequently, homeowners are now guided towards alternatives such as porcelain, sintered stone, natural stone, laminate, stainless steel, and solid timber, alongside compliant low-silica options.

The suitability of a material depends on the project scope. A benchtop refresh in an existing layout involves different considerations than a full rebuild where cabinetry and appliance positions are also changing. Enquire about the templating process, order timelines, included edge profiles, and whether installation is covered by the quote. A prudent approach involves comparing materials based on their suitability, availability, and the total quoted scope, rather than making assumptions about performance or cost.
